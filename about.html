About OneSec

The compliance layer behind your business.

OneSec is the company secretarial division of Oneacc Consulting — built for business owners who want their CIPC filings, share registers, and statutory compliance handled properly, without having to become an expert in it themselves.

Working alongside Oneacc's tax and accounting practice, OneSec covers the company-registry and administrative side: registrations, annual returns, director and beneficial-ownership changes, BEE certificates, and the Department of Labour and SARS registrations every business needs to stay in good standing.
